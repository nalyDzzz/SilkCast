Direction B · Broadcast guide

On Air

IPTV is television, so this direction borrows broadcast's own tools: channel numbers, a programme guide, lower-thirds, a clock, and the remote-control focus box. Type is large and hyper-legible so the app works with arrow keys and from across the room, and each of its three accent colours means one thing: yellow is where you are, red is what's on air, cyan is time.

Signature: a red NOW line through every channel's timeline, and a yellow focus box that follows your arrow keys.
