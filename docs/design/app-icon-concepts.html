SilkCast · App icon · Mulberry + On Air

Three icon concepts

Each one uses the Mulberry palette and is drawn twice: a detailed master for 48 px and up, and a simplified drawing for 16–32 px. That's where the taskbar, title bar and tray actually show it.

1 · Thread Recommended

A silk thread pulled from a hairline into an italic S. It ends in the glowing bead the app uses on every progress bar, so the icon and the UI share one signature. It's a direct descendant of today's ribbon.

2 · Cocoon

A play mark wound from thread around a lit core, like a silk cocoon. The rings also read as a signal going out. It's the most obviously a video app, and it's the closest to every other player's icon.

3 · On Air

A channel bug: a broadcast-signage S with an on-air tally light, taken from the B wordmark. It's the most legible at 16 px and the most TV, but it has the least silk in it.

If we go ahead

  • The master becomes an SVG in the repo, exported to a 1024 px PNG. Then npx tauri icon generates the .ico, .icns and Store PNGs in src-tauri/icons.
  • The hand-simplified 16/24/32 px drawings get packed into icon.ico next to the generated sizes. tauri icon would otherwise just shrink the master, and the bead and highlight would turn to mush.
  • The same mark becomes the in-app wordmark (icon + Bodoni italic "SilkCast"), the favicon, and the tray icon.
  • It's a full-bleed rounded tile, so it holds up on any wallpaper or taskbar. Today's grey ribbon all but disappears on a light taskbar.
